## Overview
Develop a website for Destination Pagoda. This website is to support the Destination Pagoda proposal which is in turn part of the Gardens of stone proposal. The differences between 
this website and the Gardens of Stone website is:

* Emphasises why Lithgow is a great destination and why there should be investment into Lithgow tourism infrastructure and the investment into the Destination Pagoda plan
* Does not emphasise campaigning

Successful tourism based marketing emphasises services as well as destinations. It is the destination that people travel for, but it is also the services that add to the community income and make the trip generally pleasurable. People empathise with people who look like they are having a good time and settings that they aspire to be part of their image.
